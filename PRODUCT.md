# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Luqa is primarily for its owner, Luca, in the middle of everyday life: planning
and recording time, checking habits, logging a gym session, splitting an
expense, or reflecting on longer-term patterns. Most interactions happen on an
Android phone and need to work quickly with one hand. iOS is a secondary target
and should feel at home where inexpensive platform adaptations are available.

## Product Purpose

Luqa is a private personal operating system. It brings daily capture and
long-term reflection into one coherent place so that recording life takes
seconds while understanding it can span weeks or years. Success means the app
is fast and trustworthy enough to use throughout the day, yet rich enough to
reveal useful patterns without feeling clinical or administrative.

## Positioning

One personal system that turns everyday activity into a useful, evolving record
of life without the friction or impersonality of a general-purpose tracker.

## Brand Personality

Bold, precise, modern, and personal. Luqa should feel technically capable and
confident, with restrained playfulness that makes it clearly someone's own
space rather than enterprise software. Trade Republic is a reference for its
decisive hierarchy, stark contrast, typographic scale, and restraint—not for its
financial metaphors or impersonal tone. Personality comes through in thoughtful
details, responsive motion, language, and meaningful moments—not decoration or
gamification.

## Anti-references

Luqa should not resemble a corporate SaaS dashboard, a neon cyberpunk console,
a childish gamified habit app, or a collection of excessively rounded cards and
pills. It should avoid gratuitous dashboards, decorative glass effects,
constant celebration, and color used without meaning.

## Design Principles

1. **Capture at the speed of thought.** Common actions are obvious, immediate,
   and comfortable with one hand.
2. **Make history feel alive.** Data should accumulate into useful context and
   reflection rather than static administration.
3. **Personal, not performative.** Delight is quiet and specific to the owner's
   data; the interface never imitates social or productivity theatre.
4. **Precision earns trust.** Money, time, health, and progress states are
   legible, consistent, and explicit about errors or uncertainty.
5. **Calm until attention is useful.** Neutral surfaces carry the interface;
   color and motion appear when they identify, explain, or confirm something.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target strong WCAG AA contrast, robust system text scaling, generous touch
targets, screen-reader semantics, and reduced-motion behavior. Color is never
the sole carrier of identity or status. Dark and light modes are equally
supported, with semantic tokens tuned independently for each rather than one
theme being a mechanical inversion of the other.
